How to play

One button does everything: Space, Up, W, a mouse click, or a tap. Pass through a gate to change something about the run.

Forms

Cube
Press to jump. Hold to keep jumping. Tap glowing rings in mid-air for an extra jump.
Mech
The longer you hold, the higher it jumps. Tap for a short hop under low ceilings.
Glider
Hold to climb, let go to sink.
Hopper
Each press gives a short hop, even in mid-air.
Dart
Hold to rise at a steep angle, release to dive.
Swing
Each press reverses which way you fall.
Roller
Press on a surface to flip gravity and drift across.
Blink
Press on a surface to warp straight to the opposite one.

Gates

Form gate
Shows the icon of the form you’re about to become.
Mirror gate
Flips the whole screen so you run the other way.
Gravity gate
Sends the Cube or Mech to run along the ceiling, and back.
Speed gate
More chevrons means faster.

Modes

Courses
Twelve themed tracks from gentle to brutal.
Practice
C places a checkpoint, X removes the last. Gates add one automatically.
Easy mode
Crash and you respawn a moment earlier instead of back at the start. Switch it on from the menu or the course list.
Endless
A fresh course every run that keeps speeding up.
Daily run
An endless course that’s the same for everyone today.

Esc or P pauses. R restarts.

The 3D graphics use WebGPU. Browsers without it show the flat version of the game, and you can switch to flat graphics from the menu on slower devices.

Account

You sign in with the Google account or email you use for Claude, so there’s no separate password. Pick a player name once and your best results are saved to it and shown on the leaderboard. Your email is never shown to other players.
